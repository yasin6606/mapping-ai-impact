The Impact of AI on the Environment and Energy Consumption

Group Insights · Yasin · Bahar · Pantea · Ferdaws

Data centres already draw hundreds of terawatt-hours. AI is the fastest-growing slice. This site maps electricity demand and environmental pressure using IEA annex figures and open generation data.

1. Project Description

Group Insights documents two linked effects of AI: energy consumption (how much electricity data centres use) and environmental impact (emissions intensity, water for cooling, and pressure on grids). Numbers come mainly from the IEA Energy and AI data annex and from national generation statistics (Ember).

Core data sources

  • IEA data annex (Energy and AI) — installed capacity, PUE, load factor, electricity consumption by segment and region, with Base / Lift-Off / High Efficiency / Headwinds scenarios to 2030–2035
  • Ember yearly generation — electricity mix and CO₂ intensity by country and source (context for grid carbon intensity)
  • IEA Key Questions on Energy and AI
  • Stanford AI Index 2026 — complementary training and capacity estimates

Two parts of the story

  • Energy consumption — total electricity, IT vs facility overhead (PUE), hyperscale vs enterprise, regional split (US, China, Europe).
  • Environment — emissions intensity of the grids that supply data centres, water for cooling, and scenario risk if demand grows under Lift-Off assumptions.

2. Visualisation pages

Three focused pages. All charts are pure SVG styled only with CSS classes.

Energy consumption

World data-centre electricity: 269 TWh (2020) → 416 TWh (2024) → 946 TWh (Base 2030). Capacity, PUE, load factor, and four IEA scenarios. Regional share: United States ~44% of 2024 consumption.

Water & cooling

Why cooling matters, operator WUE ranges, and how electricity intensity on different grids multiplies the water footprint beyond on-site use.

Environment & carbon

Grid carbon intensity, training-scale emissions, and how High Efficiency vs Lift-Off scenarios change the environmental load by 2030.

3. Method

  1. Open data only — IEA annex + Ember generation series.
  2. SVG charts styled exclusively via CSS selectors (no inline styles).
  3. Semantic HTML5; Roboto Slab (titles) + Fira Sans (body).
  4. Light / dark theme toggle (preference stored in the browser).
