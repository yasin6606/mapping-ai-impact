Mapping the Impact of AI through Data

Group Lumen · Yasin · Bahar · Pantea

A quiet atlas of numbers that reveal how artificial intelligence is rewriting the map of work, research and everyday life. Built from open data so anyone can follow the threads.

1. Project Description

This website is our contribution to the University of Genova course project Mapping the Impact of AI through Data. We set out to turn dense statistical reports into something you can actually read and explore — clear visual stories that show where AI is already present and where its shadow is still growing.

We draw from five public sources that speak different languages of the same phenomenon: the Stanford AI Index tracks the frontier of capability and capital; Eurostat records how European enterprises are adopting the technology; Our World in Data follows the long arc of research and compute; OECD.AI and the Anthropic Economic Index add policy and labour-market texture. Together they form a living map rather than a single snapshot.

Data at hand

  • Stanford HAI AI Index Report 2026 — model performance, investment, incidents, public opinion
  • Our World in Data – Artificial Intelligence — publications, patents, compute, funding
  • Eurostat isoc_eb_ai — AI use by EU enterprises (size class & country)
  • Anthropic Economic Index — labour-market exposure signals
  • OECD.AI — policy, incidents and international comparisons

Key snapshot (2025 data)

  • 20 % of EU enterprises (10+ employees) used at least one AI technology
  • 55 % of large EU enterprises adopted AI
  • US private AI investment reached $285.9 billion
  • Industry produced >90 % of notable frontier models
  • Documented AI incidents rose to 362

2. Visualisation Pages

Each secondary page opens a different window onto the same landscape. New pages will appear roughly every week as the course unfolds.

Enterprise Adoption across Europe

How many companies in each country have already invited AI into their daily work? A bar chart drawn from Eurostat shows Denmark leading at 42 %, the EU average resting at 20 %, and the clear gap between large and small firms.

Investment & Compute Scale

Where the money flows, the models grow. This page traces the surge in private capital and the rising energy appetite of training runs, contrasting American investment leadership with other global patterns.

Research Output & Model Transparency

Who publishes, who builds the most capable systems, and how open are those systems becoming? A look at publication volume, industry dominance and the declining transparency scores of frontier models.

3. Method & Principles

  1. Open data only — every figure links back to a public source so the map can be verified.
  2. SVG + CSS selectors — charts are pure SVG styled exclusively through the external stylesheet (no inline styles).
  3. Accessible structure — semantic HTML5, clear heading hierarchy and keyboard-friendly navigation.
  4. University identity — typography (Roboto Slab for titles, Fira Sans for text) and a colour palette inspired by UniGe visual guidelines.
