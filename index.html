Mapping the Impact of AI through Data

Group: Neural Cartographers · Team members: Yasin (lead) · Research Agent · Design Agent · Implementation Agent

An interactive exploration of how artificial intelligence is reshaping economies, research, and society — grounded in open data from Stanford HAI, Eurostat, Our World in Data, OECD and Anthropic.

1. Project Description

This website is the living deliverable of the University of Genova course project Mapping the Impact of AI through Data. Our goal is to transform complex, multi-source statistics into clear, interactive visual narratives that help students, policymakers and citizens understand the real-world footprint of AI.

We deliberately combine global and European lenses: the Stanford AI Index provides frontier capability and investment signals, Eurostat captures enterprise adoption across EU member states, Our World in Data supplies long-term research and compute trends, while OECD.ai and Anthropic’s Economic Index add policy and labour-market texture.

Data at hand

  • Stanford HAI AI Index Report 2026 — model performance, investment, incidents, public opinion
  • Our World in Data – Artificial Intelligence — publications, patents, compute, funding
  • Eurostat isoc_eb_ai — AI use by EU enterprises (size class & country)
  • Anthropic Economic Index — labour-market exposure signals
  • OECD.AI — policy, incidents and international comparisons

Key snapshot (2025 data)

  • 20 % of EU enterprises (10+ employees) used at least one AI technology
  • 55 % of large EU enterprises adopted AI
  • US private AI investment reached $285.9 billion
  • Industry produced >90 % of notable frontier models
  • Documented AI incidents rose to 362

2. Visualisation Pages

Each secondary page focuses on a distinct visualisation family. New pages will be added roughly every week as the course progresses.

Enterprise Adoption across Europe

Interactive bar and choropleth-style views of Eurostat isoc_eb_ai data. Compare countries, size classes and year-over-year growth. Highlights Denmark (42 %), Finland (37.8 %) and the EU average of 20 %.

Investment & Compute Scale

Explores the explosion of private capital and training compute. Contrasts US leadership in private dollars with China’s dominance in publication volume and patent grants.

Research Output & Model Transparency

Tracks scholarly publications, notable model releases, and the declining transparency of the most capable systems (Foundation Model Transparency Index).

3. Method & Principles

  1. Open data only — every figure links back to a public source.
  2. SVG + CSS selectors — charts are pure SVG styled exclusively via external CSS (no inline styles).
  3. Accessible structure — semantic HTML5, proper heading hierarchy, keyboard-friendly navigation.
  4. University identity — typography (Roboto Slab + Fira Sans) and colour palette inspired by UniGe visual guidelines.
