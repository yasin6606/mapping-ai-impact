The Impact of AI on the Environment and Energy Consumption

Group Insights · Pantea Rezaei · Bahareh BaradaranSalmas · Yasin Gourkani · Ferdaws Ghanney

Data Visualization · University of Genoa. A guided look at data-centre electricity, AI’s uncertain share, and why location changes the carbon story.

Main question

How is the growth of AI contributing to data centre electricity demand, and how does the location of this infrastructure shape its potential environmental impact?

We explore changes in electricity demand, where data centres are concentrated, and the electricity sources available in those places. We distinguish total data-centre consumption from estimates that try to isolate AI.

Intended audience

Primary readers are non-specialist university students—especially in computing and data science—who use AI tools and want to understand environmental implications. We assume familiarity with basic charts, not with power systems or carbon accounting. Educators and science communicators are a secondary audience.

The site uses plain language, consistent units, short explanations next to charts, and visible sources. The goal is informed understanding—not a precise footprint for one prompt or advice on where to build a data centre.
