Investment & Compute Scale

Stanford AI Index 2026 · private capital & training compute

Where money gathers, models grow taller. In 2025 American private investment reached $285.9 billion — more than twenty times the reported private figure for China — while the global total climbed past half a trillion dollars.

Key figures

  • Global corporate AI investment 2025: $581.7 B (+130 % year-on-year)
  • US private AI investment: $285.9 B
  • China private (reported): $12.4 B
  • Industry share of notable models: >90 %

Why it matters

Capital concentration shapes which capabilities become available, at what price, and under which rules. The same data also reveal a growing environmental footprint: training emissions and data-centre power demand rise in parallel with investment.
