Carbon & everyday scale

Scenario risk · Intensity · Familiar comparisons

Carbon impact ≈ electricity volume × grid intensity. Scenarios change the volume term; location changes the intensity term. Below: pathway risk and order-of-magnitude comparisons.

Scenario volume risk (2030)

IEA-style pathways span roughly 669 TWh (Headwinds) to 1 264 TWh (Lift-Off). Base sits near 946–950 TWh. The High Efficiency vs Lift-Off gap is on the order of 500 TWh—large enough to matter for national power systems if supplied by fossil plant.

Rounded TWh from IEA annex-style sensitivity cases.

Everyday scale (illustrative)

Comparisons help non-specialists feel the size of the numbers. They are order-of-magnitude only and always label whether the figure is all data centres or an AI-related estimate.

  • ~416 TWh / year (all data centres, recent) is in the same ballpark as the annual electricity use of a medium-sized European country.
  • ~950 TWh / year (Base 2030) approaches a few percent of global electricity.
  • A single frontier training run can be reported in tens of GWh to low hundreds of GWh depending on model and methodology—still small next to annual fleet demand, but concentrated in time and place.
  • Per-query energy for large models is often reported in tenths of a Wh to tens of Wh; totals grow with usage volume, not with one prompt alone.

Reading rule

  • Always check: all data centres vs AI-specific; IT vs full facility; year; region.
  • Prefer ranges and scenarios to one headline percentage.
  • Location multiplies every TWh by a different gCO₂e factor (see Europe page).
