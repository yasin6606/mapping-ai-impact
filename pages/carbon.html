Environment & carbon

Emissions intensity · scenario risk · grid context

Carbon impact = electricity volume × grid intensity. IEA scenarios set the volume range; Ember generation data shows how clean or dirty each country’s mix is.

Volume risk: four 2030 pathways

World data-centre electricity in 2030: Headwinds 669 TWh · High Efficiency 792 · Base 946 · Lift-Off 1 264 TWh. The gap between High Efficiency and Lift-Off is almost 500 TWh — enough to change national emissions paths if supplied by fossil plant.

Intensity: the other half

Ember’s generation series shows large differences in gCO₂e/kWh by country. A data centre on a hydro- or nuclear-heavy grid emits far less per TWh than one on a coal-heavy grid. Training emissions (tens of thousands of tonnes CO₂e for one frontier model) sit on top of continuous operational load.

Environmental takeaway

  • Volume is set by capacity growth, utilisation and PUE — IEA scenarios quantify the range.
  • Intensity is set by the local power mix — Ember data make country differences visible.
  • Levers: efficiency and workload placement (volume); clean power PPAs and siting (intensity). Both matter under Lift-Off demand.
