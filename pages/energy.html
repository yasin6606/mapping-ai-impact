Energy Demand

Electricity for data centres and AI workloads

In 2025 global data centres used about 485 TWh of electricity. Facilities built for AI grew 50% in a single year and already account for roughly one third of that total.

The scale of demand

According to the International Energy Agency, electricity consumption from data centres rose 17% in 2025. The AI-focused slice grew much faster. Power capacity dedicated to AI has reached about 29.6 GW — comparable to the peak demand of New York State.

What drives the growth

Training runs for frontier models now require multi-megawatt clusters for weeks or months. Inference — answering everyday prompts — already accounts for a large and rising share of total AI electricity because it runs continuously at global scale. Efficiency per query is improving, yet total volume is rising faster.
