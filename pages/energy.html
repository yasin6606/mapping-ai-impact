Takeaway

  • US + China dominate absolute TWh today and in Base 2030.
  • PUE is improving (especially hyperscale), but volume growth still outruns efficiency in Base and Lift-Off.
  • High Efficiency keeps 2030 under 800 TWh; Lift-Off exceeds 1 200 TWh.
