Energy consumption

Data-centre electricity · IEA Energy and AI annex

World data centres used 416 TWh in 2024 (up from 269 TWh in 2020). Under the IEA Base case that figure rises to about 946 TWh by 2030. Lift-Off reaches 1 264 TWh; High Efficiency stays near 792 TWh.

Capacity, PUE and load factor

Installed capacity rose from 60 GW (2020) to 97 GW (2024). IT capacity moved from 38 GW to 68 GW. Global average PUE improved from 1.53 to 1.41; hyperscale sites already operate near 1.14.

Where the demand sits

In 2024 the United States alone accounted for about 183 TWh of data-centre electricity (roughly 44% of the world total). China ~102 TWh; Europe ~68 TWh. Under the Base case, US consumption approaches 426 TWh by 2030 and China 277 TWh.

Takeaway

  • Demand is concentrated: US + China already dominate absolute TWh.
  • PUE is improving, especially at hyperscale, but absolute growth still outruns efficiency gains in the Base and Lift-Off cases.
  • High Efficiency keeps 2030 consumption under 800 TWh; Lift-Off pushes past 1 200 TWh.
