Electricity mix visualizations

Our World in Data / Ember electricity mix · European focus · 2024 (and 2020 for change)

Nine chart types built from the same generation data (TWh by source). Used to compare grids that supply data centres: fossil vs low-carbon shares shape the carbon cost of each TWh.

1. Bar chart — Wind generation 2024 (TWh)

Simple ranking of wind output in selected European countries. Germany leads this sample.

2. Grouped bar chart — Wind vs solar 2024 (TWh)

Side-by-side comparison of two low-carbon sources that often co-locate with new digital load.

3. Heat map — Source intensity by country (normalized row shares)

Each row is a country; cells show that source’s share of the country’s total generation (darker = higher share). Nuclear-heavy France and hydro-heavy Norway stand out.

4. 100% stacked bar — Generation mix shares 2024

Every bar totals 100%. Compare structure of the mix, not absolute TWh.

5. Waffle chart — Germany 2024 mix (1 cell ≈ 1%)

100 cells. Quick reading of fossil vs wind/solar/bio vs residual.

6. Circle packing — Absolute generation by source (selected countries, 2024)

Area ∝ TWh. Nested by country then source (top sources only for readability).

7. Dumbbell plot — Coal TWh 2020 → 2024

Change in coal generation. Germany and Poland remain high; UK and others fall sharply.

8. Stacked bar — Absolute mix 2024 (TWh)

Same countries; stack height = total generation. France’s nuclear block dominates the sample.

9. Pie chart — United Kingdom 2024 generation mix

One country snapshot. Gas and wind are the two largest slices in this year.

Source & notes

  • Data: electricity-mix series (Ember / Our World in Data style), generation in TWh by source.
  • Year shown is 2024 unless noted (dumbbell uses 2020 and 2024).
  • Facility counts ≠ TWh. These charts describe the grid mix that multiplies data-centre demand into emissions.
  • Charts: D3.js SVG. Theme toggle recolors axes/labels.
