Electricity mix visualizations

Data from electricity-mix.csv (Ember / Our World in Data) · Europe · 2024

Nine chart types from the same generation file (TWh by source). Grid mix multiplies data-centre demand into emissions.

1. Bar chart — Wind generation 2024 (TWh)

Ranking of wind output. Germany leads this European sample (141.6 TWh).

2. Grouped bar chart — Wind vs solar 2024 (TWh)

Side-by-side comparison of two low-carbon sources.

3. Heat map — Source share by country (row-normalized)

Each cell = that source’s share of the country’s total generation. Darker = higher share.

4. 100% stacked bar — Generation mix shares 2024

Every bar totals 100%. Compare structure of the mix, not absolute TWh.

5. Waffle chart — Germany 2024 mix (1 cell ≈ 1%)

100 cells from CSV totals for Germany.

6. Circle packing — Absolute generation by source (2024)

Area proportional to TWh. Nested by country, then source.

7. Dumbbell plot — Coal TWh 2020 → 2024

Exact CSV values. Coral = 2020, teal = 2024.

8. Stacked bar — Absolute mix 2024 (TWh)

Stack height = total generation. France’s nuclear block dominates.

9. Pie chart — United Kingdom 2024 generation mix

From CSV: gas 86.3 and wind 83.3 TWh are the two largest slices.
