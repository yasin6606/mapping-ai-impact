Water & cooling

Environmental side of heat rejection

Electricity that is not free-air cooled needs water on site or at the power plant. As data-centre load grows, water demand scales—unless cooling design and grid mix improve.

From PUE to water

A global average PUE near 1.41 means a large share of site power is cooling and other overhead. Hyperscale sites often report PUE closer to 1.1–1.2. Water-use effectiveness (WUE) for large operators commonly falls between about 0.12 and 0.9 litres per kWh of IT energy, depending on climate and design.

Indirect water

Power plants withdraw and consume water too. On many grids, water embedded in electricity exceeds on-site cooling. So the AI water footprint is both a cooling problem and a grid-mix problem. Dry cooling, free air, and clean power cut both.

Link to demand scenarios

  • Higher TWh pathways (Base, Lift-Off) raise water pressure if WUE is unchanged.
  • High Efficiency and better PUE reduce the bill before new cooling tech arrives.
  • Location (cool climate + clean grid) multiplies the benefit.
